CART444-2026: RUBRIC BREAKDOWN Rubric for Design System Evaluation This rubric serves as a communication tool between us, articulating expectations and requirements of each project, and the evaluation criteria I will use in grading. I will grade each category of the rubric and provide feedback. If you have any questions, check in with me about your grades or other concerns during office hours or by making an appointment. Key Evaluation Areas  • Principles: The identification and elucidation of Design System Principles & Philosophy, how do these principles guide and encapsulate the essence of your Design System. • Visual Design: The depth of study and consistency with regards to grid, spacing, responsive design, aspect ratio, typography, layering, colour and components. • Component Library: The depth of study and completeness of atoms, molecules, organisms and flexibility pertaining to the Design System. • Documentation: Ensure clear usage guidelines and code snippets are available. • Systemisation: The system scales well across different mediums.  • Accessibility: Ensure that standard accessibility guidelines are considered and adopted where applicable in the Design System (e.g., WCAG).  Grading Scale Definitions  • A (Exemplary): Functional, reliable, easy to use, and establishes unique and coherent design language (cohesion). • B (Proficient): Generally functional and usable, but with minor gaps in foundation scaffold (cohesion) in documentation or consistency. • C (Developing): Functional but requires significant cognitive load or effort to use due to inconsistency. • D/F (Inadequate): Difficult to use, high rate of inconsistency, does not establish a coherent design language across different mediums.
