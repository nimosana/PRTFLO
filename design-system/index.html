Design System

Principles

My design system follows the philosophy of the Adobe Spectrum and IBM carbon references provided, the following sections are designed to make the website navigation as accessible and easy to use. Using flexible and responsive layouts to ensure it correctly displays across different devices and aspect ratios. It also keeps a consistent and relatively simple structure, and follows the WCAG guidelines to ensure it is accessible to screen readers. Components use reusable classes to maintain coherence and scalability. The structure of the website and interactive elements are kept simple to ensure navigation remains intuitive. States of interactive elements, hovers and click interactions are made obvious to users of varying levels of computer literacy. Spacing, grids and organization also remain consistent and balanced to clearly separate and group different elements.

Grid

Components can be placed in grid rows or flex layouts to group elements and create order and structure. The .grid-auto class automatically fits columns (minimum 250px) into the available space. This allows larger displays to have more columns, while ensuring smaller displays don't have thin, silly looking columns.

Responsive/Adaptive design breakpoints

Layouts adapt smoothly for mobile screens below 768px using a responsive breakpoint. At this size, spacing is reduced to create a tighter vertical rhythm, and containers use less padding to better fit smaller displays. Interactive elements like buttons expand to fill the available width, making them easier to tap. Multi-column layouts automatically collapse into a single column as the screen narrows, ensuring content remains readable and well-structured.

Layers

The system utilizes elevated translucent surface elements to layer content over the fixed background, creating a pronounced sense of depth. This glassmorphism aesthetic is achieved by combining a semi-transparent base with a strong background blur. Elements are further pulled forward using drop shadows and border effects. Additionally, Headers use a subtle shadow and glow to stand out and make the website feel more polished.

Colors

The foundation is dark, highlighting accent actions. Tints, backgrounds, and text variations ensure accessibility contrast requirements are consistently met.

Typography

The chosen fonts naturally complement each other to create a cohesive aesthetic, the underlying code automatically uses Aldrich for headings and Electrolize for body text, to differentiate their hierarchy, and to keep the code simple and scalable. The fonts were chosen for their futuristic but clean aesthetic.

Aspect Ratio

Images conform to specific ratios such as 16:9, 4:3 and 1:1, these ratios are the most commonly used in the digital space, and consequently in my works, consistency across the website is key to creating a cohesive aesthetic.

Icons and Iconography

In this design system, iconography is utilized to provide clear visual symbols that represent key ideas, actions, or narratives directly relating to the portfolio's content. By using universally understood icons in place of or alongside text, they simplify complex concepts and enable immediate, intuitive interactivity without taking up unnecessary space. These icons provide a simple and highly effective way to communicate information at a glance, ensuring that the navigation remains accessible while simultaneously enhancing the overall futuristic aesthetic.

Components

Interactive elements follow WCAG contrast and keyboard navigation standards. Focus states are clearly visible across the site, making it easy for keyboard users to track their position. Text colors are chosen for strong readability against the background. Buttons and other interactive elements also provide clear visual feedback on click, and subtly lift and deepen their shadow on hover.
