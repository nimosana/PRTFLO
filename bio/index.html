Statement

Being of Colombian descent, growing up in a working-class neighborhood in Montreal, and witnessing struggles in both countries has made me acutely aware of the injustices that exist, and of far greater ones across the world. An awareness of my relative privilege drives my commitment to open dialogue and to draw attention to overlooked, misunderstood, or suppressed perspectives and situations.

My goal as an artist is to use my technical skills and knowledge to explore, discuss, and highlight societal issues. I am particularly interested in how systems of power, media, and information shape public perception and construct dominant narratives, as well as the role digital platforms play in shaping how these realities are perceived.

I aim to create experiences that not only inform but also provoke critical engagement, encouraging audiences to reconsider the systems and narratives that may shape their understanding of the world.

Biography

Montreal-born of Colombian descent, Nicolas is a predominantly digital artist who spent most of his childhood behind computer and console screens. Throughout elementary and high school, he repeatedly stayed up late trying to hack web or console games. His rebellious nature and curiosity about technology fostered early skills and a deeper understanding of different digital systems and how they operate.

While studying computer science in CEGEP, he went beyond experimenting with existing systems toward creating his own projects, simultaneously developing a strong interest in 3D printing, beginning to design and model his own objects. Co-developing a Java-based game as a thesis project with friends, he discovered a lasting interest in computation arts and the creation of interactive experiences, including games and digital artworks.

Today, as an undergraduate student at Concordia University's Specialization in Computation Arts, he's taken this opportunity to expand his technical skillset, developing projects across web platforms and game engines such as Unreal Engine and Unity. He has continued to expand his 3D practice, moving beyond printing into animation and broader 3D media. Access to professional photography and cinema equipment through his studies sparked a growing interest in photography and video production, which have become important parts of his creative practice.
